# Source-data scope and external attribution

This is the formally adopted saved-scalar supplement in the local revised article
package. It has not been uploaded to an external data deposit. Only synthetic
scalar experiment records, saved-only external synthetic scalar references, and
previously permitted anonymous real-data aggregates are included. The private
122-segment case records, reserve-model geometry, coordinates, occupancy and
probability arrays, case-specific adapters and weights are excluded. The package
cannot independently reproduce the confidential real-case evaluation.

The external porphyry reference uses [exepulveda/geomet_datasets](https://github.com/exepulveda/geomet_datasets)
at commit `46e5f9cab80c6d69a412ceb4cd07ecc46bbed423` and the study by Garrido,
Sepulveda, Ortiz and Townley (2020), *Simulation of Synthetic Exploration and
Geometallurgical Database of Porphyry Copper Deposits for Educational Purposes*,
Natural Resources Research 29, 3527–3545,
[DOI 10.1007/s11053-020-09692-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11053-020-09692-6).
The repository states [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).
The three `files/external150/` CSVs are derived scalar evaluation outputs from one
external synthetic parent; preserve this source attribution and applicable
upstream terms on redistribution. This adoption does not relicense them under a
general code or manuscript license. No original or modified NC-SA block model,
drillhole data, geometry, raw assays or prepared occupancy/observation arrays are
copied. Upstream inputs and licensed geometry must be obtained separately under
their source terms and kept outside this submission/code tree.

The 150 external scalar rows are dependent five-cutoff/three-mask conditions from
one synthetic parent, not independent deposits or newly generated public
predictions. All levels and unfavorable outcomes remain in the files.

The recorded software environment reports scikit-mps 0.6.0. No separate native
GENESIM executable version was recorded, and the two version concepts are not
equated. No physical coordinate unit is asserted for the confidential real data.
All real outcomes are descriptive aggregates from related segments in one mining
area. Source paths in the index and input-hash ledger are author-archive provenance
identifiers; their presence does not authorize access or redistribution of the
underlying restricted artifacts.
